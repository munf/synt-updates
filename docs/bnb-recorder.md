# BNB recorder

synt records new token launches on BNB Chain from two launchpads, four.meme and Flap, and follows what happens to them in their first minutes.

## What it records

- Each launch, who launched it, and its bonding-curve state.
- The token state 120 seconds after launch, which is the point the outcome labels use.
- Behaviour across launches, such as the same token name launched by many different creators (clone waves) or one creator launching many tokens in a day.

Data notes describe behaviour in aggregate. They never name a wallet.

## Why the numbers are provisional

The outcome labels for BNB are not calibrated yet. Calibration needs enough labelled winners and at least 14 days of continuous data. Until then:

- every BNB number in a data note is marked provisional, and
- there are no accuracy claims for BNB scores.
